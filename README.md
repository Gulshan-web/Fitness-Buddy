# 🏋️ Fitness Buddy

**Fitness Buddy** is an AI-powered fitness and wellness assistant that helps users maintain a healthy lifestyle through personalized fitness guidance.

It provides workout suggestions, healthy meal ideas, motivation, and habit-building support based on the user's goals, fitness level, preferences, and available time.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 💬 AI Fitness & Wellness Chatbot
- 🏋️ Personalized Home Workout Suggestions
- 🥗 Healthy Meal & Nutrition Ideas
- 💪 Fitness Goal Guidance
- 🔥 Daily Motivation & Fitness Tips
- 📅 Habit-Building Support
- 👤 Personalized User Profile
- 📊 Fitness Dashboard
- 🤖 AI-Powered Recommendations
- 📱 Responsive and User-Friendly Interface

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many people find it difficult to maintain a healthy lifestyle because of limited time, lack of personalized guidance, and inconsistent motivation.

**Fitness Buddy** solves this problem by providing an accessible AI fitness coach that users can interact with anytime.

---

## 💡 Solution

Fitness Buddy works as a virtual fitness and wellness coach.

Users can provide their fitness goals and preferences, and the AI assistant provides suitable:

- 🏋️ Workout recommendations
- 🥗 Meal and nutrition suggestions
- 🔥 Motivation
- 📅 Habit-building guidance
- 💪 Healthy lifestyle recommendations

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. User opens Fitness Buddy.
2. User provides their fitness goals and preferences.
3. The AI understands the user's requirements.
4. **IBM Granite** generates personalized guidance.
5. **Watson Orchestrate** coordinates the required AI tasks.
6. User receives workout, nutrition, motivation, or wellness recommendations.

---

## 🌟 Innovation

The main innovation is **personalized AI fitness guidance in one platform**.

Instead of providing the same advice to everyone, Fitness Buddy adapts recommendations according to the user's:

- Fitness goals
- Fitness level
- Preferences
- Available time

This makes fitness guidance more personalized, accessible, and user-friendly.
